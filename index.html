Metric definitions
  • Nodes visited: search positions entered, including terminal and cutoff positions.
  • Leaves: positions evaluated at terminal states or at the depth horizon.
  • Cutoffs: Alpha-Beta bound events where remaining siblings were skipped after α ≥ β. The lab trace records ply, node type, column, bounds, and skipped sibling count.
  • Depth: maximum number of plies reached from the root.
  • Duration: wall-clock milliseconds measured around the search.
